# Public Research

GitHub is the research lab; these files are the log of taking questions to practitioners.

Transcripts live in
[`../evidence/practitioner-responses.md`](../evidence/practitioner-responses.md). The method
is in [`../research/08-practitioner-research.md`](../research/08-practitioner-research.md).
These files record **what was posted, where, and what came back**.

## The rule

> **No fabricated human responses.**

Nothing may be recorded as a practitioner response unless it is a real reply from a real
person, with a link. Specifically forbidden:

- paraphrasing a response from memory
- reconstructing what someone "basically said"
- inventing an illustrative or representative response
- generating a plausible response with an AI tool
- adding a placeholder response to demonstrate a format
- treating a response to a different question as a response to ours

An assumption may not move to `CHALLENGED` or `SUPPORTED` in
[`../decisions/assumption-log.md`](../decisions/assumption-log.md) on the strength of a
response that does not exist. This is the rule the entire Tier 3 layer rests on: the moment
one invented response enters the record, none of the others can be trusted either.

Where a response is known to exist but has not been transcribed, it is marked
`⚠ AWAITING TRANSCRIPTION` with the text left empty — never filled in provisionally.

## Posting rules

1. **One research question per contribution.** Do not bundle.
2. **Never post the same text to two communities.** Adapt to the audience or do not post.
3. **Ask, do not pitch.** The project is context. Do not lead with the agent.
4. **State that nothing is built.** Every post says so.
5. **Check community rules first.** Verify each subreddit's self-promotion policy before
   posting.
6. **Do not argue in-thread.** A reply that challenges an assumption is the deliverable.
   Record it. Disagree in the repository, not at the respondent.
7. **Record silence.** A post that draws nothing is a finding about reach — log it.

## Attribution

Respondents are quoted under their public handles with links to the original public
comments, which is how the claim stays checkable. No respondent is described as endorsing
the project, and no stated background is embellished. Anyone who asks to be removed is
removed, and the finding that rested on them is marked as unsupported rather than quietly
retained.
